# Approval policy

Read-only inspection and bounded research may proceed within stated limits. Require explicit approval before writing project files, changing requirements, using secrets, paid or remote compute, uploading data or artifacts, changing visibility, sending notifications, deployment, destructive cleanup, or autonomous retries.

Approval records actor, scope, target, cost ceiling, data exposure, expiry, and observed outcome. One approval does not authorize a later operation or a different target. If an operation is unavailable, approval authorizes only the proposed handoff, never an invented success.
